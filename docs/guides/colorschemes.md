# Colorschemes

Open **Settings → Appearance** and choose **System**, **Light**, or **Dark**. System follows your operating system's appearance. Choose a palette for light mode and another for dark mode.

## Included palettes

- **Hibi light / dark** use neutral backgrounds and a teal accent.
- **VS Code Light Modern / Dark Modern** use neutral editor colors and blue accents.
- **Catppuccin Latte / Frappé / Macchiato / Mocha** offer one light and three dark pastel palettes with mauve accents.
- **Nord** uses dark blue-gray backgrounds and cool blue accents.

Your choice applies throughout Hibi and stays after restarting. Changing colors keeps the document and undo history intact. If you disable an addon that provides your selected palette, Hibi uses its default colors until you enable it again.

## Exported documentation

[Documentation exports](exporting.md) include all nine built-in palettes and your appearance preferences. Readers can use the appearance button to choose their own colors. Their saved browser preference takes precedence over yours and works offline.

Exports do not include custom addon palettes or CSS. Select a built-in palette before exporting to keep the same colors in the result.
